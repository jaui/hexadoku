# Puzzle designed by Claude Ghyselen, who names it in 2012 as his own
# first for Elektor, ahead of the Hexamurai 7-8/2009, the Hexadocube
# 7-8/2010 and the EUC Penta-Hexadoku 7-8/2012.
#
# 25x25 cells over 25 symbols: the numbers 1 through 9 and the letters A
# through P - "including the letter 'O', hence the absence of the '0' in
# the numbers", which is how a 25-symbol alphabet stays readable. Each
# row, column and 5x5 box holds every symbol once.
#
# On top of that: "the puzzle is essentially a (16x16) Alphadoku
# comprising nine classic (9x9) Sudokus using numbers 1 through 9. ...
# The nine embedded Sudokus have a background colour." Measured off the
# page render, the shading covers nine 3x3 blocks, one in the middle of
# each of the nine middle 5x5 boxes - rows and columns 7-9, 12-14 and
# 17-19. Every clue inside them is a digit, and each block holds 1-9
# once; see alpha.go for why that is the whole of the extra rule.
#
